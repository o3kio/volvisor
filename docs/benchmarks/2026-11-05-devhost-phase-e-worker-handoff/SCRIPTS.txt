Measurement caveat
------------------
This is a single-host, loop-backed-LVM control-plane run, not production
capacity, SLA, durability or data-path evidence. The timed mixed sweep and
the soak contain no create/delete calls, so their deltas are not attributed
to the measured change. Create/delete appear only in untimed
setup/cleanup and the concurrency-1 micro phase. The c128 tier is
variance-dominated on this host: a single sample cannot support a gain or a
regression claim. The 24-hour soak is NOT RUN. See REPORT.md sections 4
and 10.
